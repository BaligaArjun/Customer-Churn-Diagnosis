# 📊 Customer Churn Diagnosis

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project focused on identifying customer churn patterns, quantifying revenue loss, and uncovering the key drivers behind customer attrition through SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

---

# Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for any business because losing existing customers is often more expensive than acquiring new ones. Although the company had access to customer transactions and support data, it lacked visibility into why customers were leaving, how much revenue was being lost, and which customer segments required immediate attention.

This project was built to transform raw business data into an interactive decision-support dashboard that helps management monitor churn trends, identify financial risks, and prioritize customer retention efforts.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Measure overall customer churn and retention.
- Calculate the financial impact of customer churn.
- Identify the major reasons customers leave.
- Analyze churn across different sales channels.
- Compare customer lifetime revenue between active and churned customers.
- Detect cities with the highest revenue exposure.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

# Key Business Insights

- The dataset recorded a **94.37% churn rate**, indicating significant customer retention challenges.
- More than **₹5 Billion** in revenue was identified as being at risk due to customer churn.
- Churned customers represented a substantial share of the company's customer lifetime revenue, highlighting the long-term financial consequences of customer attrition.
- Jaipur recorded the highest revenue exposure among all cities, making it the highest-priority location for customer retention initiatives.
- Revenue loss remained consistently high across multiple years, demonstrating that churn was an ongoing business problem rather than a temporary issue.
- Different sales channels contributed differently to churn, allowing management to identify which customer acquisition strategies required improvement.
- Customer support issues and service-related factors emerged as major contributors to customer loss.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

- Strengthen customer support processes to reduce service-related churn.
- Launch targeted retention campaigns in high-risk cities.
- Focus loyalty programs on high-value customers with high lifetime revenue.
- Improve customer experience within high-risk sales channels.
- Monitor churn KPIs monthly through executive dashboards.
- Investigate product and pricing issues contributing to customer dissatisfaction.
